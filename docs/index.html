How to read this dashboard. Recommendations = qualifying funding extremes (|fund_z| > 1.0) with trend confirmation, ranked by composite magnitude. fund_z = annualised funding ÷ 10d realised vol — interpret as Sharpe-of-carry: how many vol units you are paid (or paying) to hold the perp. Cross-sectionally comparable across XAU at 15% vol and a memecoin at 200% vol; raw funding alone is not. Conviction tier ★★★ = top decile, ★★ = next quartile, ★ = rest of qualifying. Stop = nearest 7d swing in trade-against direction. 14d carry = % of notional captured if held delta-neutral 14 days. Funding alone is not a thesis — pair with an independent fundamental view. Position sizing should assume a 2–3 day forced-unwind window for TradFi perps given delisting precedent. Liquidity filter: 24h volume ≥ $5M USDT. Refresh: twice daily at 08:00 and 20:00 SGT via local Task Scheduler.
