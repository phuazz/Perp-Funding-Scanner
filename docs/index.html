How to read this dashboard. Recommendations = qualifying funding extremes (|fund_z| > 1.0) with trend confirmation, ranked by |fund_z| × max(|trend|/6, 0.5) × (1 + |Δz|). fund_z = annualised funding ÷ 10d realised vol — Sharpe-of-carry, cross-sectionally comparable across XAU at 15% vol and a memecoin at 200% vol. Δz = fund_z_7d − fund_z_30d — vol-normalised positioning acceleration; bigger |Δz| means crowdedness is fresh and vulnerable to mean reversion (vs chronic / structural). Conviction tier ★★★ = top decile, ★★ = next quartile, ★ = rest of qualifying. Stop = nearest 7d swing in trade-against direction. 14d carry = % of notional captured if held delta-neutral 14 days. Funding alone is not a thesis — pair with an independent fundamental view. Position sizing should assume a 2–3 day forced-unwind window for TradFi perps given delisting precedent. Liquidity filter: 24h volume ≥ $5M USDT. Refresh: twice daily at 08:00 and 20:00 SGT via local Task Scheduler.
